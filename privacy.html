Privacy Policy for Pagos Sevres

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection and Access

Pagos Sevres requests access to your Gmail data strictly to read and process emails automatically as authorized by you. We only request the minimum necessary permissions required to execute these automated tasks.

2. Data Usage and Sharing

All data accessed through Google OAuth scopes is used solely to power the core automation features of the Pagos Sevres platform. We do not transfer, sell, or share this data with third parties, external servers, or human operators. Your data is never used for advertising, tracking, or user profiling.

3. Google Limited Use Disclosure

Pagos Sevres' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your email data is kept completely private. No email contents or personal information are permanently stored on our infrastructure; data is processed entirely in real-time memory.
